Claude (free or paid)

  1. Open claude.ai → Settings → Connectors.
  2. Click Add custom connector.
  3. Name it learnmcp and paste your URL.
  4. Click Add. In a new chat, enable learnmcp in the tools menu.
  5. Try: "What are my lessons?"

The free plan allows one custom connector.

ChatGPT (Plus/Pro, web)

  1. Go to Settings → Apps → Advanced settings and turn on Developer mode.
  2. Click Create app, name it learnmcp and paste your URL.
  3. Choose No authentication, confirm and create.
  4. In a new chat, select learnmcp from the tools menu.
  5. Try: "Quiz me on lesson 1"
